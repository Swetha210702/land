My Co-Founded Venture

Inside
TIQWorld
in motion.

I walked in carrying a problem most people never notice. Behind every aircraft, refinery, railway, and energy plant, there are people working silently to keep them safe, and the world of testing, inspection, and NDE that holds it together is traditional, fragmented, and digitally underserved. TIQWorld is my attempt to change that.

The recognition came alongside it. TIQWorld was named among the Top 50 Emerging Changemakers for FY 2025-26 at the IITM Incubation Cell, and we received our first grant that took no equity, through the Techpioneer initiative supported by American Express and HDFC Bank. We incorporated only in August 2025, so all of this still feels early.

These are checkpoints, not endpoints. Grateful to Prof. Krishnan Balasubramaniam, the CNDE ecosystem, and everyone who believed before there was anything to show. Even more excited for what comes next.
