About Me

Researcher by day
Founder by deadline Educator by choice.

I’m Srijan Tiwari — a researcher, founder, speaker, and educator from IIT Madras. I don’t see entrepreneurship as just building a company; I see it as building systems that outlast conversations. My work sits where AI, NDE 4.0, manufacturing quality, certification, and skill verification meet.

I’ve cleared my way into India’s top technical institutions, chosen the research-and-startup route, and built TIQWorld to solve a problem I kept seeing up close: industry needs trusted talent, students need real exposure, and the quality ecosystem needs a digital layer that connects both with credibility.
